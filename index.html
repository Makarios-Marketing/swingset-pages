Why it matters

Why Protect Your Playset Early?

A new cedar swing set looks great, but Midwest weather starts working against it from day one. Sun exposure can fade and weather the wood, while rain, snow, moisture, and repeated freezing and thawing can accelerate its natural aging.

A premium oil-based cedar protectant helps slow that process by penetrating and protecting the wood from the elements. The goal is not to make an old swing set look new again. It is to help keep a newer swing set from looking old before its time.

Good questions

Frequently Asked Questions About the Refresh & Protection Program

How often does a Fall Refresh happen?

That depends on your plan. We recommend applying the clear protectant once or twice a year depending on sun and weather exposure. Annual plans put it on a set schedule so you never have to track it yourself.

What if my set needs a repair during a visit?

We inspect the hardware and wood on every visit, tighten what needs it, and replace worn or missing hardware at no extra cost. If something larger needs attention, we let you know before doing any extra work.

Do I need to be home during the visit?

No. Payment is handled ahead of time, so you do not need to be home. We check first that there are no bad dates for your family, and we send photos once the Refresh is complete.

Do you offer custom stain colors?

No. This is a protection service, not staining or refurbishment. We use a clear, oil-based protectant that soaks into the cedar to guard against moisture, UV, and Midwest weather. The goal is to protect and extend the life of your set, not to change its color.

What areas do you cover?

We serve families across Illinois, Indiana, Michigan, and Wisconsin. Reach out and we will confirm your address is in range.
